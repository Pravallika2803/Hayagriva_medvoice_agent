## 📞 How Your Project Matches ElevenLabs' Healthcare Suite

Instead of an in-person doctor assistant, think of your system as the **Automated Patient Inbound & Telehealth Router** processing incoming calls for a hospital or a large insurance center like Optum.

### 1. The Voice Input

* **The Action:** A patient or a pharmacy rep calls the hospital line. Your microphone capture handles the live stream, and **Whisper** converts the audio transcript to text.

### 2. The Intent Router (The Core Logic)

Your LangChain Intent Router takes the incoming transcribed spoken request and determines where it needs to go:

| Spoken Intent | The Route Taken | The Source Data |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *"Hey, I need to reschedule my appointment for tomorrow afternoon."* | ➡️ **SQL Route** | **SQLite:** Updates the structured clinic calendar, patient ID profile, and available slots. |
| *"Can you tell me if this medication has specific dietary warnings according to the NIH?"* | ➡️ **RAG Route** | **FAISS:** Searches official medical documentation PDFs (CDC/NIH/WHO charts) for instant information retrieval. |
| *"I need to refill my prescription for Drug X, but I'm currently experiencing mild nausea."* | ➡️ **HYBRID Route** | **SQLite + FAISS:** Queries SQLite to see if they have active refills left, while simultaneously querying FAISS to see if nausea is an expected side effect of Drug X. |

### 3. The Validation Layer & Output Loop

The local Mistral GGUF processes the context assembly, packs it into a clean structured JSON format, and pipes it straight into the **Text-to-Speech (TTS)** framework. The caller hears a completely natural voice response answering their scheduling or medical policy question seamlessly.
